Rooted in Denmark, connected to the world, shaped by Peru

How can we make a better country and/or even a better world?

Back into my young adulthood, I had that question going around in my head. Here I am, with the same question, but fortunately with more answers than at that time.

Engineering is one of the answers, and that is the one I decided to pursue. Over the years, I have followed that path.

With good luck, I found friends, superb working places, nice colleagues, and a great family that supported and still supports that journey.

Free time

More free time, I like to go for a run or ride the bike in the forest. And with free time in between, I like reading or listening music and podcasts.

Computers became another hobby. I am not into gaming, but I must confess, I surrendered a bit to MineCraft.
